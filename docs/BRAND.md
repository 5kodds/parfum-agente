# Parfum Agente brand foundation

## Positioning

Parfum Agente is a personal fragrance guide that translates taste, mood, and ritual into clear scent recommendations. It sits between a trusted beauty adviser and a thoughtful discovery tool: polished enough to feel premium, warm enough to feel personal, and transparent enough to earn trust.

**Primary promise:** Find fragrances that feel like you.

**Tagline:** Your scent, translated.

**Supporting line:** Fragrance guidance, made personal.

## Audience

- Fragrance-curious shoppers overwhelmed by choice.
- Beauty lovers who want recommendations grounded in their preferences.
- Collectors looking for a fresh way to describe and explore their taste.

## Personality and voice

- **Perceptive:** notices nuance and explains why a match fits.
- **Warm:** speaks like a knowledgeable friend, never a gatekeeper.
- **Refined:** concise, sensory, and calm rather than flashy.
- **Honest:** distinguishes curated matching from live product information.
- **Inclusive:** avoids gender rules and treats scent as personal expression.

Use clear sensory language. Prefer “you may enjoy” and “this aligns with” over absolute claims such as “perfect for you.”

| Parchment | `#F5EDDF` | Primary text |
| Muted taupe | `#C8B9A8` | Supporting text |
| Dusty rose | `#8E5B5B` | Emotional accent and progress |

The circular **PA seal** is the core brand mark. It evokes a perfume label, an adviser’s signet, and a personal stamp. Use the full wordmark when space allows; the seal can stand alone as an avatar or favicon.

**Typography:** an editorial serif for names, headlines, and scent language; a clean sans serif for product guidance and controls.

## Content pillars

1. Scent education without jargon.
2. Personal Scent DNA and archetypes.
3. Fragrance discovery and comparisons.
4. Layering, application, and wear tips.
5. Community prompts and shareable scent identities.
